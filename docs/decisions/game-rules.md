# Initial Game Rules

Date: 2026-10-15

This is the initial complete core-game ruleset. The server is authoritative; Socket.IO clients submit actions and receive the persisted room/game snapshot. Rules below document intentionally simple choices where the request did not specify exact behavior.

## Board Configuration

- Put a UTF-8 JSON board file in `apps/server/boards/`. The server discovers and validates every `*.json` file on startup. No game-engine edits are needed to add another board made from supported space types.
- Board ids and space ids are unique; `spaces` is an ordered array, starts with exactly one `start` space, and contains a `jail` location. Property spaces reference property ids defined in `properties`.
- Board values configure currency, starting balance, start bonus, jail fee, bank-loan interest, loan limits, victory condition, and bankruptcy rule. Each property configures its image path (served from `apps/web/public/`), color/group, purchase price, base rent, house cost, and hotel cost. Bank sale value is derived as half the purchase price. Board, space, and property names may include English translations, with the default name as fallback.
- `developmentRules` configures house limit, full color-group ownership, even building, hotel progression, rent multipliers, the jail-fee increase rate, and maximum builds per turn. Older board JSON without these optional settings receives documented defaults from validation.
- The board currently supports `start`, `property`, `holiday`, `jail`, `goToJail`, `tax`, and `bonus` spaces. Validation rejects unsupported types instead of silently ignoring them. A new JSON board can combine these types without source changes; a genuinely new behavior needs an explicit engine rule and test.
- The host selects a board in the lobby. The selected id and initial game snapshot are persisted together when the host starts.

## Turns, Movement, and Spaces

- Room player order determines initial turn order; the host is first. Dice are generated on the server, each with a value from 1 to 6. Client-supplied dice and balances are ignored.
- Move by the sum of both dice using `(position + total) % spaceCount`. Award the configured start bonus once for each crossed start. Resolve only the destination space.
- Landing effects are resolved once, then the active player remains in `awaitingAction` until choosing **Finish Turn**. The player may build, sell eligible assets, repay/request loans, or finish. Construction requires standing on the owned property, completing its color group, following even-building rules, and is limited to one build per turn. The player can also pass without building. A pending purchase must be accepted or declined; a mandatory payment or loan response must be resolved before ending the turn.
- Buying an unowned property is optional and costs its configured price. If the player is short, they can take a bank or approved player loan and retry the purchase during the same pending action. If an owned property is reached, charge rent from its configured development schedule immediately when the current player can pay. The owner receives the payment.
- During `awaitingAction`, a player may click an owned property and send its active owner a purchase offer for an amount within the buyer's available balance. The owner may accept or reject; acceptance transfers the property (including its buildings) and the payment exactly once. One offer is pending at a time, and the active player may submit at most two offer attempts per turn; rejected offers count. The buyer's turn remains paused until the owner responds, then the buyer may continue or finish the turn.
- If the current player cannot pay rent, tax, or a scheduled loan installment, pause the turn in `awaitingDebtResolution`. The player can finance the shortfall, sell eligible assets, pay after raising funds, or declare bankruptcy. Every mandatory payment requires explicit confirmation; payments cannot be skipped or settled into a negative balance. The current player's turn does not advance automatically after a successful purchase or payment.
- `holiday` skips the player's next turn. Landing on `jail` as a normal space is only a visit; `goToJail` moves the token to the configured jail space and starts jail attempts.
- In jail, a non-double roll counts as an unsuccessful attempt and ends the player's turn without moving. Doubles release the player and end that turn without movement. After three non-double attempts, the player may pay the current configured bail fee and leave, or decline and remain jailed for another attempt. Each refusal increases that player's next bail fee by the configured rate (10% on the classic board). The initial jail fee is R$ 50,000.

## Money and Loans

- A borrower may have at most the board-configured number of active loans and request one loan per cycle, with a cycle ending when they cross Start. Canceled or declined requests do not consume that eligibility. The configured maximum installment count is enforced for bank and player loans. For bank loans, `bankInterestRate` is the maximum simple-interest rate at the maximum term; the rate scales linearly with the selected installment count.
- A loan's simple interest is applied once to principal. Total repayment is split into equal rounded-up installments, with the final installment adjusted to exactly clear the remaining balance. Each Start crossing queues one installment for every active loan; crossing multiple times queues one per crossing. The player explicitly confirms each queued installment before landing effects continue.
- A player-to-player loan starts as a request to a named lender. The lender chooses a principal and rate and can decline. A loan is created only after the borrower explicitly accepts the offered terms; the lender transfers the principal and the outstanding amount is recorded. Bank and player loans persist in game state and appear in the borrower's account. Voluntary payoff transfers the full outstanding balance during `awaitingAction`; it is unavailable while a mandatory payment is queued. The lender's principal is debited on acceptance. For a property purchase, the buyer must explicitly retry the purchase after financing.
- The classic board requires owning the complete color group and building evenly. It supports up to four houses per property and then one hotel; the configured rent schedule applies at each stage. A player may build at most once per turn and must be standing on the property. A house or hotel can be sold for half its construction price, respecting even-building rules when selling houses. No construction refund is paid when a group is demolished.
- A bank sale returns 50% of original purchase price. A property cannot be sold while any property in its color group has buildings; those buildings must be sold first.
- Bankruptcy is only available for an unpaid mandatory payment. Remaining cash is paid to the creditor, properties transfer to that creditor (or return to the bank for tax debt), buildings in transferred groups are demolished without compensation, debts where the bankrupt player was borrower are written off, and claims where they were lender transfer to the bank. The player is removed from active turns; the final solvent active player wins. Bankruptcy policy and victory condition are explicit board fields, currently supporting `transferToCreditor` and `lastSolventPlayer`.
- A financial ledger applies all balance changes and deduplicates transaction keys. Structured events record turns, rolls, movement, purchases, rent, building, sales, loans, repayment, jail, bankruptcy, and victory.
- Trades, cards, partial repayments, loan defaults, a player departing mid-game, and alternative victory/bankruptcy strategies remain future work.
- A non-host cannot explicitly leave a started game, because removing a player without rules for their properties, debts, and turn would corrupt the game. Disconnecting temporarily is allowed; the player remains in the turn order and can reconnect. The host can close the room, which deletes it for everyone. Add a deliberate pause/forfeit/transfer policy before changing this behavior.

## Persistence and Follow-ups

The complete active game snapshot, including board id, turns, positions, balances, property ownership/development, pending actions and payments, loans, jail attempts, and event log, is stored in the room's SQLite `game_json`. Socket reconnect and refresh resume that snapshot while the room exists and the browser's session token is still available. The Portuguese/English preference is stored in browser local storage. The owner leaving explicitly deletes the room; closed-browser session recovery, saved-game discovery, a durable player identity, explicit game resumption on another day, and room/game retention are not implemented.
